# Chapter 3 — AI for Requirements & Product Analysis

## Goal

Turn the accepted RideShareHub brief and context into testable requirements and detailed feature behavior without making architecture or interface decisions.

## Workflow

1. Draft and review the PRD.
2. Human accepts, rejects or defers review findings and approves the PRD.
3. Draft and review the Feature Specification.
4. Human approves success, validation, failure, recovery, persistence and authorization behavior.

## Rules

- Do not invent product decisions or change V1 scope.
- Keep unresolved choices as open questions.
- Do not choose technology, API, schema or UI.
- Only the human may mark an artifact `Accepted`.

## Outputs

- `docs/product-requirements.md`
- `docs/feature-specification.md`

Chapter 4 may be prepared as a draft, but it is not canonical until Chapter 3 is accepted.
