# Chapter 4 — AI for Product Design

## Goal

Translate accepted RideShareHub behavior into a reviewable interaction contract, comparable visual directions and a disposable static prototype without changing product scope.

## Workflow

1. Draft and approve the design brief.
2. Inspect references and create three comparable concepts.
3. Human selects/refines a direction.
4. Build and audit the prototype.
5. Human approves the final design record.

## Rules

- Requirements and Feature Specification outrank visual preference.
- Prototype conventions are not production decisions.
- Include success, empty, validation, failure, recovery, keyboard, narrow-view and reduced-motion states.
- Only the human may mark artifacts `Accepted`.

## Outputs

`docs/`, `design/user-flow.mmd`, three `design/concepts/`, `prototype/` and review evidence.
